# IT equipment

Policy owner: Workplace IT · Version 2.3

## 1. Standard equipment

| Role family | Laptop |
|---|---|
| Software development, data | ThinkPad T16 32 GB or MacBook Pro 14 |
| Branch and business roles | ThinkPad T14 16 GB |

Peripherals (monitor, headset, docking station) are requested with an IT ticket.

## 2. Delivery

2.1. Laptops are delivered to the reception desk of your site, normally by day 3 or 4.

2.2. Track the shipment in the portal. If the laptop has not arrived **one business day after** the expected delivery date, open a ticket to the **IT** queue.

2.3. Until your laptop arrives you can use a loaner at the IT desk (floor 2 in Milano, back office in branches).

## 3. Accounts and security

3.1. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts from day 1.

3.2. VPN access is activated once the corporate laptop is enrolled. Personal devices cannot connect to internal systems.

3.3. Never install unapproved software; request it through the Software Catalog.
