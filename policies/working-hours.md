# Working hours and smart working

Policy owner: HR · Version 3.0

1. The working week is **37.5 hours**, Monday to Friday.
2. Flexible start between 08:00 and 10:00; core hours are 10:00 to 16:00.
3. Smart working: up to **8 days per month** after the first 30 days. During the first 30 days, at most 1 day per week, agreed with your manager.
4. Overtime must be approved in advance by the manager and is recorded in the HR system.
5. Public holidays follow the national calendar plus the patron saint day of your site (Milano: 7 December, Sant'Ambrogio; Torino: 24 June, San Giovanni).
