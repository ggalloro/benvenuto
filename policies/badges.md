# Badges and building access

Policy owner: Corporate Security · Version 1.6

## 1. Getting your badge

1.1. Upload a passport-style photo in the portal **by day 2** of your onboarding. Printing starts once the photo is approved and takes 2 business days.

1.2. Until your badge is ready you receive a **temporary pass**, valid for 10 days. Collect it at the reception desk with an ID document.

## 2. Tiers

| Tier | Access |
|---|---|
| standard | Office floors of your assigned site |
| branch | Your branch, including the counter area |
| restricted_areas | Standard plus labs and data center areas; requires a Security ticket approved by the manager |

## 3. Lost, stolen or damaged badges

3.1. Report a lost or stolen badge within 24 hours by opening a ticket to the **Security** queue. The badge is suspended immediately.

3.2. A damaged badge (cracked, bent, unreadable chip) is replaced for free the first time. Attach a photo of the damaged badge to the Security ticket.

## 4. Rules

Wear your badge visibly. Do not lend it. Do not let people without a badge follow you through the gates (tailgating).
