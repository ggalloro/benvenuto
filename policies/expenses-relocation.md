# Relocation expenses

Policy owner: HR and Payroll · Version 4.1 · Applies to new hires who relocate at the company's request.

## 1. Eligibility window

1.1. Eligible expenses are those incurred **from 30 days before to 90 days after the start date**. Expenses dated outside this window are not reimbursed.

1.2. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date.

## 2. Limits per category

| Category | Code | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Travel to the new location | `travel` | €300 per trip, max 2 trips | Second class train or economy flight, for the employee only |
| Temporary housing | `temporary_housing` | €120 per night, max 30 nights | Hotel or serviced apartment while you look for a home |
| Moving services | `moving_services` | €1,500 in total | Professional movers; two quotes required above €1,000 |
| Meals during temporary housing | `meals` | €40 per day | Only on nights covered by temporary housing |
| Other | `other` | Pre-approval by HR | Open an HR ticket before spending |

2.1. The overall cap for relocation expenses is **€5,000** per employee.

2.2. A claim above a category limit is **not** submitted through the portal. Either submit it for the limit amount, or open an HR ticket to request an exception, attaching the receipt.

## 3. Receipts

3.1. Every claim needs a receipt or invoice that shows: vendor name, date, itemised amount and the vendor's VAT number (Partita IVA for Italian vendors).

3.2. A clear photo of a paper receipt is accepted. Credit card slips alone are not receipts.

3.3. The receipt date is the expense date used for the eligibility window.

## 4. Non-reimbursable items

Fines, alcohol, minibar, personal entertainment, deposits for rental contracts, furniture purchases.
