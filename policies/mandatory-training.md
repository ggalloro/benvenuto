# Mandatory training

Policy owner: Compliance · Version 2.0

## 1. Required courses for every new hire

| Course id | Course | Duration | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| GDPR-101 | Privacy and GDPR essentials | 2 h | Within 10 days of the start date |
| INFOSEC-101 | Information Security fundamentals | 3 h | Within 10 days of the start date |
| AML-101 | Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing | 4 h | Within 14 days of the start date |

The deadline for each employee is shown in the portal as `due_date`.

## 2. Booking a session

2.1. Courses run as scheduled sessions, online or in person. Book a session with available seats through the portal.

2.2. Prefer a session that ends **before** your deadline. If none is available, book the first available one and inform your manager.

2.3. You can cancel up to 48 hours before the session.

## 3. Consequences

3.1. Missing courses block access to the systems that require them (see Access to internal systems, section 2).

3.2. After the deadline, Compliance notifies the line manager every week until the course is completed.

3.3. AML-101 is a regulatory requirement (Bank of Italy supervisory provisions). It cannot be waived.
